Guest Information

Thank you for choosing to stay at Parkside Motel. We hope you have an enjoyable and pleasant stay as our guest(s).

Reception Hours

Reception hours are from 8.00am – 9.30pm.

If, for any reason reception is not staffed during these times, please call 5243 6766.

We advise that after close of reception, only emergencies, urgent matters & noise disturbances will be attended to.

If you lock yourself out of your room after 9.30pm you will incur a $50 call out fee.

Check‑In & Check‑Out

Check‑in: 2:00pm

Check‑out: 10:00am

Servicing of Rooms

Rooms are stripped and cleaned on guest departure.

For multiple night stays, rooms are serviced daily between 10am - 2pm.

Please report any spillages or accidental damage to reception immediately.

Guests are responsible for any spillage, loss or damage to the appliances, furniture and fixtures and all fittings in the room, fair wear and tear is expected.

Where professional cleaning is required the guest will be responsible for the costs.

Visitors to your room

Only registered guests are permitted in your room.

For the safety and security of all our guests, visitors are strictly prohibited from entering building/rooms at any time.

No parties or large groups of people are permitted in the rooms.

Parkside Motel has a strict NO PARTY POLICY.

Guests will be asked to leave the property if this policy is not adhered to.

Exceptions can be made for special circumstances by prior arrangement only.

Please speak to reception to arrange and ensure any visitors are registered on arrival.

Privacy

Parkside Motel respects your privacy

A “Do not disturb” sign is provided in your room.

Please place the sign on the outside of your room door should you require privacy.

While all care is taken to respect your privacy, there are times where your room may be accessed regardless of the presence of a sign (emergencies, urgent maintenance, if we believe house rules or laws have been violated)

Where possible, all attempts will be made to contact you first.

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

Keys

Lost or unreturned keys will incur a $350 replacement fee.

Non-Smoking Policy

Parkside Motel has a strict NO SMOKING POLICY in all rooms, inside buidling & in public toilets.

Please respect this for the comfort of other guests

A cost of $500 will be charged to your account/credit card if the policy is not adhered to.

This is to cover the cleaning of carpets, curtains, cushions, blankets and throws.

If smoking within the building triggers a fire alarm a minimum cost of $1000 will apply for emergency services call out.

There is provision for people wishing to smoke with seating & metal ashtrays to the right as you enter the under cover carpark.

Cooking

Strictly No cooking in rooms.

Guests found to be cooking in rooms will incur a $500 fee.

If the fire alarm is activated a minimum fee of $1000 will apply for emergency services call out.

Noise

In consideration of other guests, Parkside Motel has a strict No Party Policy.

No loud noise is permitted after 9.00pm.

Parkside Motel prides itself on providing a homely and relaxing environment for the benefit of all guests.

Please ensure that your television and other appliances are at a comfortable level so as not to disturb other guests.

If at any time you are disturbed by noise from within the property please contact the onsite manager on 5243 6766 for assistance.

Pets

Pets are not permitted in the rooms

Registered service dogs with accreditation documents are accepted

This does not include emotional support animals.

Guests found with pets in their rooms will be charged $500 cleaning fee

Guest Laundry

The laundry is located on the ground floor around the corner from the lift in the under-cover parking area.

There is a washing machine and dryer and washing powder for guest use (please note a fee applies)

Please see reception for access

The laundry is accessible from 8am and is locked at 8pm daily

If you require dry cleaning, Crest Dry Cleaning located at 132 High Street, Belmont or alternatively Spin n Span Dry Cleaners located at 46c Roslyn Road, Belmont
