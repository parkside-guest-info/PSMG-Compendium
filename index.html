Fire Evacuation Procedures

The Parkside Motel is equipped with comprehensive fire detection and prevention systems.

We ask that you familiarise yourself with the “Emergency Evacuation Plan” located on the back of your room door.

You will also find “Emergency Evacuation Plans” at each end of the corridors on level 1 and level 2.

In the event of a fire, the emergency warning bell will sound.

If you should become aware of a fire or smell smoke, please notify reception immediately and state the location in order for us to investigate.

In the event of a fire, please follow these procedures:

1.Remain calm and do not panic.

2. If you are in your room, check the door for heat before opening it. If the door is hot, do not open it and stay inside.

3. If the door is cool, open it slowly and check for smoke or fire in the corridor. If it is safe to exit, leave the room immediately.

DO NOT BRING LUGGAGE

4. Use the nearest exit to evacuate the building.

DO NOT USE ELEVATORS.

5. Once outside, move to the designated assembly point and wait for further instructions from emergency personnel.

6. Do not re-enter the building until it has been declared safe by the authorities.

7. Follow any additional instructions provided by emergency personnel.

If you are unable to leave your room:

* All motel doors are fire rated in accordance with the Australian Fire Safety Standards

* Stay calm and call reception immediately to inform them of your situation.

* Use wet towels or clothing to block any gaps around the door to prevent smoke from entering the room.

* Open a window to allow fresh air in, but be cautious of smoke entering from outside.

* Signal for help by hanging sheets out of the window or using a flashlight to attract attention.

* Do not attempt to climb out of the window.

* Remain close to the floor with a damp cloth over your nose and mouth.

* Wait for emergency personnel to arrive and follow their instructions.
