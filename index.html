Reception hours are from 8.00am – 9.30pm. If, for any reason reception is not staffed during these times, please call 5243 6766. We advise that after close of reception, only emergencies, urgent matters & noise disturbances will be attended to. Servicing of Rooms

Rooms are stripped and cleaned on guest departure. For multiple night stays, rooms are serviced daily between 10am - 2pm. Please report any spillages or accidental damage to reception immediately. Guests are responsible for any spillage, loss or damage to the appliances, furniture and fixtures and all fittings in the room, fair wear and tear is expected. Where professional cleaning is required the guest will be responsible for the costs.
